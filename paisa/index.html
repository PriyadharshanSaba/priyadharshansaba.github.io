How it works

It works through your money so you don't have to

Most trackers turn you into the bookkeeper. Paisa works the other way round: it collects, checks and files things on its own, and only asks you when it isn't sure.

01 · Collects

Reads your bank mail

Paisa picks up payment alerts, card statements and rewards from your inbox with read-only access, across all your accounts.

02 · Checks

Balances the books

Each statement has to match the bank's own totals before anything is saved. The same payment is never counted twice.

03 · Files

Learns your habits

Small everyday payments go to the right category on their own, using rules it picked up from you.

04 · Asks

Asks before guessing

Anything new or large goes in a short review queue, with a suggestion ready. One tap and it remembers next time.

Trust

An accountant you can rely on

Doing things on its own only works if the numbers are right, so Paisa follows a few strict rules.

Exact to the paisa

Every amount is stored as whole paise, so ₹470.82 stays ₹470.82 and monthly totals never drift.

Spending means spending

Loans, repayments and transfers are tracked but never counted as living costs. One real month looked 66% higher before this rule.

Cautious by default

Policies are counted at their lowest band, locked money stays out of "have", and savings you haven't made yet are never assumed.
