Built so the numbers can't drift

Three rules the app never breaks, because a tracker that lies about money is worse than none.

01

Exact money

Every amount is stored as whole paise. No floats, so ₹470.82 stays ₹470.82 and the monthly total never creeps.

02

Spending means spending

Loans, repayments and transfers are tracked but never counted as living. One real month was overstated by 66% before this.

03

No guessing

If no rule matches, a transaction waits in the review queue uncategorised. Wrong labels are invisible; missing ones get fixed.

Screens shown in private mode: every figure and name is masked, so the app is safe to demo.

Goals with a deadline

Money set aside for something, by a date, highest priority first. See what's left, what to save each month, and how many months remain.

  • Priority levels

Net worth and a roadmap

What you have against what your goals need, plus what's already promised to you: policies, EPF and NPS, each at its lowest band.

  • Have vs short, goal by goal
  • Conservative, base-case roadmap
  • Savings you haven't made aren't assumed

Trends that stay honest

Month on month and category movement. Partial months are marked and left out of the average.

  • Averages over full months only
  • Category change vs last month
